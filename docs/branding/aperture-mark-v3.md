# Aperture logo v3

The v3 mark keeps the aperture motif and simplifies it for the lavender Material 3 compute workspace. The separate pieces suggest distributed workers around an open compute core. The wordmark stays live text in the UI.

- Asset: `frontend/src/assets/aperture-mark-v3.png`.
- Generated with the built-in imagegen tool; not the CLI/API fallback.
- Reference: `frontend/src/assets/aperture-mark-v2.png` (retained unchanged).
- Transparent PNG. Keep the alpha channel; do not apply a multiply blend mode.
- Usage: sidebar brand, overview illustration, footer, and favicon.

## Final generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production app logo mark for Aperture, a distributed compute workspace with a soft lavender Material 3 interface.
Input image 1: the existing Aperture logo, a reference for brand continuity. Redesign and simplify it; retain the idea of an aperture/open compute core, not its rendering.
Primary request: create one distinctive, compact geometric aperture symbol made from six broad, carefully spaced interlocking blades around a clear hexagonal negative-space opening. The connected pieces suggest distributed workers focusing into one compute gateway. Give the outer silhouette subtly rounded hexagonal proportions, friendly precision, thick forms and balanced optical weight. It must remain immediately readable at 24–48 pixels.
Style: exceptionally clean flat vector-like brand mark, crisp smooth edges, carefully balanced negative space, contemporary developer tool identity.
Palette: solid deep muted violet #6950A1 with one restrained lavender accent #9B82C7 on two of the blades. Flat solid fills only.
Composition: a single mark, centered on a square canvas, occupying about 84% of the width and height, equal transparent margins. No wordmark: the name is rendered separately in the app.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha, including the central opening and the gaps between blades. Deliver a transparent PNG; no simulated checkerboard.
Constraints: retain the aperture idea but remove all orbital lines, spheres, 3D shading, metallic material and gradients from the reference. No outlines, shadows, glow, fine circuitry, dots, text, letters, mockup, frame or presentation sheet. Original balanced geometry; do not copy another brand's logo. Only one final isolated mark.
```
